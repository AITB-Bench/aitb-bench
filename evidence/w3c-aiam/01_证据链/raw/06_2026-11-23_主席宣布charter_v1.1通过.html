Hello all,

As Chair, I confirm that charter v1.1 is adopted, effective from the 
time of this post. Six participants, listed in my post of 3 November, 
supported it with the addition to §2. No one objected, and no one 
preferred an alternative to v1.1, so I find that there is consensus.

Independent assessment

Ian Jacobs, the W3C Community Development Lead, wrote to me on 18 
November in reply to my request for an independent assessment. His 
message went only to me and to team-community-process@w3.org, so I 
summarize it here. He wrote that my post of 3 November suggests there 
is consensus and that, as Chair, I can observe the consensus and 
decide. He added that he does not think an independent perspective is 
needed if there is consensus among the participants. He also asked 
whether anyone had objected to any change in version 1.1. I told him 
that no one had. I sent him this summary before this post.

On 22 October and again on 3 November I said that I would not confirm 
the result until an independent assessment was posted here, and that I 
would abide by the assessment. Silvio asked that an independent 
assessment be recorded for this amendment, given my roles as Chair, 
author of the normative reference and implementer of it. Xinghua and 
Lucas supported asking the Community Development Lead. No assessment 
has been posted. I am confirming without one, because the Community 
Development Lead does not think an assessment is needed if there is 
consensus. This departs from what I said.

The adopted text

Charter v1.1 is the text in my call for consensus, with the sentence 
added to §2 and a line in §13 that repeats that sentence in full. The 
call is here:

  https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-ai-agent-memory-interop/2026Oct/0014.html

My post of 22 October sets out the §2 sentence. My post of 3 November 
sets out the §13 line, which replaces the shorter one in my 22 October 
post:

  https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-ai-agent-memory-interop/2026Oct/0034.html

  https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-ai-agent-memory-interop/2026Nov/0003.html

I will publish charter v1.1 on the Group's page and post here when that 
is done.

My reading of §3

In my post of 3 November I proposed words that record my reading of §3 
and set a window for comments. The window closed at the end of 20 
November anywhere on earth (12:00 UTC on 21 November). Xinghua sent the 
only reply. It raised no objection and asked for no change to §3 
itself. It made two editorial points about the words I proposed. The 
reply is archived here:

  https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-ai-agent-memory-interop/2026Nov/0007.html

Xinghua asked that my reading say the four medical-device rules are 
examples. Xinghua also noted that the phrase "the rules for NMPA 
registration review" (NMPA is China's National Medical Products 
Administration) describes a process and names no specific rule. Xinghua 
said that naming the rules or recording the phrase as a process would 
let another reader find the same rules. I accept both points. The words 
below say that the four medical-device rules are examples and record 
the NMPA phrase as a process. As Chair, I record these words as my 
reading of §3, and the Group can revisit this reading at any time:

  "I read 'comparable regimes' in §3 as any law, regulation or standard
  that sets requirements for how records are kept, protected, retained,
  corrected or erased. On that reading, the medical-device rules EU MDR
  (Regulation (EU) 2017/745), 21 CFR Part 820, ISO 13485 and the rules
  applied in the registration review process of China's National
  Medical Products Administration (NMPA) are comparable regimes.
  These four are examples. Any other law, regulation or standard that
  fits that reading is also a comparable regime."

Thank you all for taking part in this call for consensus.
