Hello all,

The call for consensus on charter v1.1 closed at the end of 2 November 
anywhere on earth. Thank you, Yasha, Xinghua, James, Lucas and Evgenii, 
for your replies since my answers of 22 October, and I am sorry that 
these answers come only after the close.

Yasha, Xinghua, Jeongho, James, Lucas and Evgenii support option 1 with 
the addition to §2, and Yasha confirmed that the addition did not 
change that support. No one preferred option 2 or another document, and 
no one objected. Lucas's reply came in the catalogue thread:

  https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-ai-agent-memory-interop/2026Oct/0064.html

Independent assessment

No participant was proposed by 27 October to assess the outcome. 
Xinghua asked me to take the fallback, Lucas supports it, and Evgenii 
proposed no name. I have now asked the W3C Community Development Lead, 
through team-community-process@w3.org, to assess the outcome or to name 
someone who will, and to post the assessment here. I said on 22 October 
that I would do this once the window for proposals closed, and it has 
taken me longer than it should have. I will not confirm the result 
until the assessment is posted, and I will abide by it.

The §13 line

Xinghua pointed out that the §13 line about the addition leaves out 
what gives the rule its effect. If option 1 is confirmed, that line 
will repeat the §2 sentence in full:

  "Where the revision a deliverable names is superseded by one that
  changes something the deliverable asserts, the Group re-examines that
  deliverable as a decision under §9, rather than leaving it pinned to a
  text that no longer says what it said (added during the call for
  consensus)."

This is editorial and changes no requirement.

The boundary of my reading of §3

Xinghua asked that the record carry the boundary of my reading of §3 
and the test behind it, as well as its conclusion. These are the words 
I propose to record:

  "I read 'comparable regimes' in §3 as any law, regulation or standard
  that sets requirements for how records are kept, protected, retained,
  corrected or erased. On that reading, the medical-device rules EU MDR
  (Regulation (EU) 2017/745), 21 CFR Part 820, ISO 13485 and the rules
  for NMPA registration review are comparable regimes."

As Xinghua notes, in the retention entry only the two retention legs 
rest on instruments that §3 does not name. Its erasure leg cites GDPR 
Article 17(3)(b), and §3 names Article 17. I have also taken Xinghua's 
correction to the U.S. citation, which places the retention period in 
21 CFR Part 820 incorporating ISO 13485 Clause 4.2.5. Because these 
words come after the close, the window for them runs separately. If 
anyone reads §3 differently, please say so by the end of 20 November 
anywhere on earth (12:00 UTC on 21 November), and I will put the 
question to the Group. Otherwise I will record these words as my 
reading as Chair, which the Group can revisit at any time.

James's point on gaps

James asked that, when the catalogue records a use case as a gap 
against the pinned revision, the conformance pack report that criterion 
as not assessable against that revision, so that the report still shows 
it and it never counts as a pass. Evgenii agreed, noting that the 
reporting format v0.1 of the Agent Conformance and Benchmarking 
Community Group, which Evgenii edits, keeps such a state, 
"not-exercised", separate from pass and fail. This needs no change to 
the charter. I will list it for the Group's review of the pack.

Regards,
Russell Jackson
Chair, AI Agent Memory Interoperability Community Group
