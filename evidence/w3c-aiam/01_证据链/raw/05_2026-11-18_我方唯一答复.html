Russell -

I have no different reading of §3 from the words you propose to record, and
thank you for taking the §13 line and the 21 CFR Part 820 / ISO 13485
Clause 4.2.5 correction.

One recording request, editorial in effect: please record that the four
medical-device instruments are examples under the test, not a closed list -
the test being that an instrument sets requirements for how records are
kept, protected, retained, corrected or erased. Without that half-sentence,
a reader arriving later can take the four named instruments as the
definition of "comparable", which would make the test jurisdictional rather
than substantive.

Separately, and only if you want the record reproducible across
jurisdictions: "the rules for NMPA registration review" describes a process
rather than a named instrument. Naming the instrument (or recording it as a
process) would let another reader locate the same text; I do not read it as
an instrument today, so I am not proposing a name.

No objection and no request to change §3's text.
